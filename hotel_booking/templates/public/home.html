About Kutwal Dream Resort

Nestled in the heart of Haramosh Valley, 60 km from Gilgit — your gateway to Kutwal Lake and the Karakoram.

Mountain Paradise at Kutwal Valley

Kutwal Dream Resort offers comfortable accommodation for trekkers, families, and adventure seekers visiting the famous Kutwal Lake and Haramosh Peak (7,409m). Our resort provides warm hospitality, local cuisine, guided treks, and jeep transport from Sassi village.

Whether you are trekking to Kutwal Lake, exploring Haramosh Valley, or simply enjoying the alpine meadows — we make your stay unforgettable.
